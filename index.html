AI Planner: Daily Routine

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026
Android package: com.aiplanner.dailyroutine

Your choice: planning, check-offs, logs and local insights work on your device. Optional AI is off by default. When you enable and use it, information described below is sent to Google through Firebase. The current AI service uses the unpaid Gemini Developer API.

Who is responsible

Ranjeet Product Studio provides AI Planner: Daily Routine. For privacy, support or deletion questions, contact ranjeetproductstudio@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

Your repeating blocks, titles, times, days, done/skipped status, log names, amounts, notes and app settings are stored in the app's local storage. The core planner does not require an email address, password, name or phone number. Android may include local app data in your Google account's device backup if device backup is enabled; that backup is controlled by your Android and Google account settings.

Information processed for optional AI

When you enable AI help, Firebase creates a random anonymous account identifier and retrieves AI configuration and credit information. When you request an AI feature, the following data is processed:

  • Plan drafts: the text you enter to describe your week.
  • Routine tips: active block titles, start times, durations and repeating days; completion counts from the recent routine; log names, counts and numeric totals; and the app's locally generated suggestions.
  • AI account and usage: the anonymous Firebase user identifier, credit balance, credits used, account creation time, block status, and request records containing the feature, model, token counts, credit cost and time.
  • Technical identifiers: Firebase installation identifiers, App Check / Play Integrity attestation information and tokens, and technical request information such as IP addresses processed by Google's services for delivery and abuse prevention.

Log notes are excluded from AI requests. However, block titles, draft text and log names or totals may themselves reveal personal, health, fitness or financial information if you put that information in them. For example, a log's numeric total can be included in a tips request. Avoid putting sensitive, confidential or personal information in content that will be sent to the unpaid AI service. You can use the offline planner without enabling AI.

Google Firebase and Gemini

We use Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Remote Config, App Check with Play Integrity, and Firebase AI Logic connected to the Gemini Developer API. Firebase processes anonymous account, configuration, security and usage data to operate these features. Requests use encrypted connections.

Unpaid Gemini processing: under Google's current terms, submitted AI content and generated responses may be used to provide, improve and develop Google products, services and machine-learning technology. Human reviewers may read and annotate inputs and outputs. This is broader than processing solely on our behalf; do not assume AI content stays private to this app. Provider handling and retention are governed by Google's applicable terms and policies.

See Firebase privacy and security information, the Gemini API terms, and Google's Privacy Policy. AI availability can depend on the country, provider eligibility, configuration and remaining credits.

Why information is used

We use the information to provide plan drafts and routine suggestions, manage anonymous AI accounts and credits, configure features, and prevent abuse. Suggestions use your routine to personalize the response. We do not include advertising SDKs or sell your personal information. Google's separate uses for the unpaid AI service are described above.

Retention and deletion requests

Local data: delete individual logs or blocks in the app, or clear the app's storage / uninstall it to remove local data. Manage device backups separately through Android or your Google account. Turning AI off prevents new app-initiated AI feature requests; it does not erase existing server records.

Developer-controlled AI records: anonymous account information, credits and request records are retained while the AI account is used; the app does not currently automatically expire these records. To request deletion, email ranjeetproductstudio@gmail.com with the subject “AI Planner data deletion”. Please contact us before clearing app storage or uninstalling, if possible. Tell us you use AI Planner and wish to delete the anonymous AI account and associated Firestore records. Do not email your planner entries or sensitive log content.

The app does not associate the anonymous account with your email. We may need to help you identify the anonymous account before locating and deleting its records. We will explain the information needed and the outcome of the request. Deleting developer-controlled records does not necessarily delete content already retained independently by Google for the unpaid service or security/legal purposes; Google's policies apply to those copies.

Adults and sensitive content

AI Planner is intended for adults aged 18 and older and is not directed to children. The optional AI is a general planning aid. Do not use it to submit sensitive personal information or rely on it for medical, mental-health, legal or financial advice.

This privacy page

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no forms, analytics scripts or tracking cookies added by us. GitHub may process technical web-request information under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Emailing support shares the information in your email with us and our email provider.

Changes and contact

We will update this page and the date above when our practices change. Contact ranjeetproductstudio@gmail.com with questions or requests.
